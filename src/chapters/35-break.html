Dive 04-ARed team / blue team · Sep 2024 – Jan 2025

Both sides of the same lab

Five months of hands-on security on lab platforms, kept as an Obsidian vault. The defensive half followed LetsDefend and the HTB blue-team track: alert triage, phishing analysis, IOC validation, endpoint investigation and incident response, plus a couple of real malware samples pulled from bazaar.abuse.ch and taken apart by hand.

Dive 04-BAIgen · Sep – Nov 2024

A RAG agent that never leaves the machine

Three months on large language models, learned by building the plumbing rather than calling an API. AIgen indexes a folder of documents into PostgreSQL with the pgvector extension, retrieves the closest chunks for a question and hands them to a model running locally through llama.cpp on an NVIDIA GPU. A small Gradio page is the chat window. Everything runs on one Linux box with no external service.

  1. 01Documents.docx and .pdf read with LlamaIndex readers (DocxReader, PyMuPDF)
  2. 02Indexerchunks embedded with a Hugging Face sentence model; table created with a VECTOR column
  3. 03pgvectorPostgreSQL 15 + pgvector 0.8; HNSW index, cosine distance
  4. 04Retrieverembeds the question, top-k nearest nodes with scores, wrapped as a LlamaIndex retriever
  5. 05llama.cppllama-cpp-python built with GGML_CUDA; GGUF model, part of the layers offloaded to the GPU, 3.9k context window
  6. 06Gradiochat UI with history on a local port

September – November 2024
