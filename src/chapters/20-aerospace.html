Catching a drone with a drone

Role
Software developer, computer vision
When
Jun 2019 – Feb 2020
Partners
FADA-CATEC team, with ISR Lisboa; Mohamed Bin Zayed International Robotics Challenge, Abu Dhabi
Paper
Ruiz García, Caballero, González, Viguria, Ollero. “Autonomous drone with ability to track and capture an aerial target”. ICUAS 2020, pp. 32–40.
DOI 10.1109/ICUAS48674.2020.9213883 Video on YouTube

My first mission at FADA-CATEC was the vision stack for the competition: detect an aerial target from a moving drone, keep it in frame, feed its position to the guidance loop so the interceptor could track and capture it on its own. The work ran on NVIDIA Jetson boards under ROS with OpenCV and Darknet/YOLO.

The tracking-and-capture pipeline was written up afterwards for ICUAS 2020, the first of three papers from this period.

GAVIOTA built ground-to-air solutions so that drones can fly low-altitude operations beyond visual line of sight without surprising manned aircraft. My part was software on the detection side: sensing approaching aircraft and turning that into something the drone's operation could act on.

The audio-based aircraft detection system from the project was published open access in Electronics in December 2020.

Drones for first responders

Jun 2021 – Jul 2022

19 partners in 8 countries, coordinated by UPV. CATEC organised the final pilot in Linares, 17 Jun 2022.
Funding
EU Horizon 2020, grant 832576
Paper
González, Caballero, Pérez-Grau, Viguria. “Vision-based UAV Detection for Air-to-Air Neutralization”. IEEE SSRR 2021. First author.
assistance-project.eu DOI 10.1109/SSRR53300.2021.9597861 Open access on Zenodo Final pilot, Linares (photos)

ASSISTANCE put drones and sensors in the hands of first responders: UAVs that fly into a disaster area, sense gas and smoke and stream what they see back to the command post. CATEC, the main Spanish research centre for civil RPAS/UAS in the consortium, brought the aerial side. I started as a software developer on the UAV integration and, from November 2021, managed CATEC's part of the project through to the final pilot in Linares in June 2022.

The vision-based UAV detection work for air-to-air neutralisation, my first-author paper, was presented at IEEE SSRR 2021 and is open access on Zenodo.
